Chat rules & privacy

Chat rules

  1. Talk hoops, not people. Trash-talk teams all you want. No insults, threats or harassment aimed at other fans, players, coaches or officials.
  2. No hate. Nothing racist, sexist, homophobic or otherwise hateful, including slurs written creatively.
  3. No spam or ads. No links to sell things, no repeated messages, no flooding the chat.
  4. No personal information. Don't share anyone's address, phone number or private details, including your own.
  5. Keep it legal. No illegal streams, betting scams or anything sexual.
  6. You must be 13 or older to have an account.

A filter blocks common offensive words, and you can send one message every 3 seconds. Use Report on any message that breaks these rules. Admins can delete messages and remove people from the chat at any time, without warning.

Privacy policy

What we collect. To chat, you create an account with an email address, a password and a username. We store your messages, the game they were posted in and when, and any reports you send. Your password is stored securely by our account provider and we can't see it.

What others see. Your username and your messages are public to anyone viewing the game. Your email address is never shown.

How we use it. Only to run the chat: logging you in, showing messages and keeping the chat safe. We don't sell your information or use it for ads.

Who stores it. Accounts and messages are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Scores and stats come from NCAA.com; the site itself is hosted by Vercel.

Your choices. To delete your account and messages, message @collegebasketball on Instagram from the account you want removed and we'll take care of it.
